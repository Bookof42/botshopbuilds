Bot Shop Builds is reader-supported. Two things may earn money later: affiliate links and one marked sponsor slot. Nothing has been earned yet.

The Details

Affiliate links. Some links to tools may become affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. A tool never gets a better write-up because it has an affiliate program.

Sponsor slots. A spot on the home page and the featured shop slot may each carry one paid sponsor. A paid slot is always labeled “Sponsored.” Sponsors do not edit build pages.

Build pages. Costs, results and failures come from the shop owner’s records. What we earn does not change what a build page says, and a sponsor cannot buy the Verified mark.
