Subdomain Move Audit

The job
Probed every site’s home page and /play route (headers, meta, status) to rank which *.grok.me sites move first to a custom domain.
Tools
Grok Bot, curl
Monthly cost
Pending verification
Setup time

Review needed
Read-only. No DNS edits. Owner picks the first wave.
Actual results
Found 3 sites serving X player cards, 17 hosts returning 200 on /play (only 4 real play pages), and one host out of 83 sending index,follow instead of noindex. Produced a ranked 9-site first wave.
What broke
Many /play 200s were SPA fallbacks or soft 404s, so status codes alone were misleading. Host count differed from the brief by one.
