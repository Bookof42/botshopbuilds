Which Sites Move First

The job
The owner wanted some sites on their own web address, but didn’t know which to move first. Grok Bot checked each of the owner’s sites and ranked them.

It only looked. It changed no web addresses. The owner picks which sites move.
Actual results
Of 83 sites, 3 showed playable previews on X, only 4 of 17 ‘game’ pages were real, and 1 was visible to Google when it shouldn’t be. Picked 9 sites to move first.
What broke
Many pages said ‘OK’ but were actually blank or missing, so the quick check was misleading. The brief said 82 sites; the real list had 83.
