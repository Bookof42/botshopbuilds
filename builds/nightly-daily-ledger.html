Nightly Daily Ledger

The job
When one person runs a shop, a channel and a dozen sites, small promises slip. Each night around 8:30 PM CT, Grok Bot checks that what the owner asked for that day actually got done, and lists anything missed or waiting.

Review needed

Actual results
Caught a comic post that missed its scheduled 3:24 PM time, and a subscription renewal date nobody had written down (Oct 14).
What broke
Grok Bot couldn’t read the day’s chat log, so it rebuilt the list from its own notes and the day’s check-ins. A search for a receipt in email came back empty.
