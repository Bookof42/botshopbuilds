Mockup Fixer

The job
A wrong mockup shows buyers a shirt that won’t match what arrives. Grok Bot checks each product photo against the real print file. Where a generated mockup redrew the print or cut off its text, it places the real print file onto the photo and uploads the fix.
Tools
Grok Bot, Shopify, Printful
Cost
Pending verification
Setup time
Pending verification
Review needed
The owner approves the rebuilt images before they replace the old ones.
Actual results
73 apparel photos checked; 13 rebuilt or matched and uploaded; 4 white shots of the Light Cone shirt made; 16 print files redrawn with a bigger ‘* = 69’ line (1.8 times larger), 8 of them live in Printful across 4 products.
What broke
Generated mockups redrew the prints and cut off the text. Fixed by placing the real print files instead of generating again.
