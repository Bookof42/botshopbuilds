Weekly Shorts Video Pipeline

The job
Our creator channels post short videos every day. Grok Bot makes a week of them: a still image per short, then 6 second talking clips of the channel’s cartoon characters.
Tools
Grok Bot, Grok Imagine image, Grok Imagine video
Cost
Each 6 second talking clip: $0.87 per shot (actual). Source: cost log
Each 10 second animated clip: $1.41 per clip (actual). Source: cost log
One week of shorts (Oct 11): $41.79 actual, plus about $0.49 for the still images (estimated). Source: cost log
Story videos (Oct 11 week): about $2.26 a day (days 3 to 8; day 2 has no cost log) (actual). Source: story video cost logs
