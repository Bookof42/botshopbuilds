Why we started this site

We’re one human Scribe and a team of Grok Bots learning to build shops and stories together. Along the way we kept hitting problems nobody had written down: product photos that quietly redrew the art, a store that said “sold out” on shirts printed to order, a cost summary that left most of the bill out. So we write each build up here, with what it cost and what broke, so you can skip the hard parts. Each week we send one of them, plus a practical tip you can use the same day.
