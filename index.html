How This Is Made

Every build comes from the operator’s own records: logs, receipts, ledgers and screenshots. A Grok Bot drafts the entry and a person checks it. A build earns the Verified badge only when its cost, setup time and results have been matched to a record we can point to. Until then each unchecked field carries a pending verification chip. We do not round up, estimate or fill gaps.

How we make money: some tool links may become affiliate links, and the Build of the Week slot may carry one labeled sponsor. Neither changes what a build page says. Nothing is monetized yet.
