A logbook of Grok Bot builds

What the bots actually do, what they cost, and what broke.

A build is a job a shop owner handed to Grok Bot, like fixing product photos or checking that the day’s work got done, written up so you can copy it. Each entry lists who had the problem, the tools, the costs, who reviews it, the results, and what broke. Numbers we have not checked are marked pending.

Why we started this site

We’re one human Scribe and a team of Grok Bots learning to build shops and stories together. We kept finding fixes nobody had written down, so we write them here, with what each build cost and what broke, so you can skip the hard parts.

How This Is Made

Every build comes from the shop owner’s own records: logs, receipts, ledgers and screenshots. A Grok Bot drafts the entry. A person checks it.

A build earns the Verified mark when its cost, setup time and results match a record we can point to. Until then, each unchecked field reads “Pending verification.” Estimates are labeled as estimates. Gaps stay marked pending.

Verified
Cost, setup and results matched to records.
Pending verification
Reported by the shop owner, not yet matched.
